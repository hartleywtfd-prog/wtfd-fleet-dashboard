# Incident Command laptop redesign — v0.4.0

- Preserves the no-horizontal-scroll new-dispatch modal.
- Preserves one-click switching to a new dispatch.
- Preserves one-click End Incident / clear behavior.
- Consolidates secondary incident actions under a compact **Tools** menu.
- Moves **Building / Occupancy** and **Command Log** into on-demand dialogs instead of consuming permanent workspace.
- Makes the primary workspace focus on **Command Organization**, **Tactical Assignments**, and **Apparatus Bank**.
- Shows four tactical assignments per page on laptop-sized screens/data model paging rather than nine at once.
- Adds laptop-specific density and layout improvements for common 1366×768 through 1600px-wide displays without shrinking critical controls excessively.
